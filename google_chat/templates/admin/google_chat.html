How to Setup Google Chat Incoming Webhook
  1. In Google Chat, navigate to the Space or group where you want notifications.
  2. Click the Space name at the top > Apps & integrations.
  3. Click Manage webhooks > Add webhook.
  4. Give it a name (e.g., CTFd Bot) and click Save.
  5. Copy the generated Webhook URL and paste it below.
